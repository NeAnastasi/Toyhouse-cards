Kao is a calm, never smiling guy. He always looks tired and sleepy. Depression is his "faithful companion". His past was pretty bloody, and Kao hates it. More than anything, Kaonashi dreams of dying... and get some sleep. He has nightmares every day and suffers from pain. All this, however, does not prevent him from successfully working as a waiter. Sometimes he accidentally disappears from work, but the bosses are used to it and do not dismiss him. In the end, Kaonashi works out everything he missed. However, sometimes he gets confused and works more than he should, and also faints.

PAST

A long time ago, there were two islands in the world: the islands of light and darkness. Kaonashi was created to become the ruler of the island of darkness. He turned out to be a crazy four-armed creature who became a tyrant. All the objectionable were executed, all the disobedient were imprisoned. No one dared to contradict him. But after many centuries of rule, the islands were destroyed by quarreling goddesses. Was it the end?..


PRESENT

After a long time, Kao's soul was merged with a human one, and he was reborn to become the keeper of darkness. Now he lives like an ordinary person, except for some factors that exacerbate his depression.
